# ROI Lens

### Uncovering the True Economics of Marketing

A next-generation Marketing Attribution & Budget Optimization Engine designed to move beyond outdated last-click models and reveal the real drivers of growth.

#### Core Capabilities

→ Multi-Touch Attribution using Markov Chains & Shapley Value Models
→ Customer Journey Intelligence across complex conversion paths
→ Bot Traffic Detection & Data Quality Auditing
→ Channel-Level ROI, CPA & Incrementality Analysis
→ Predictive Budget Reallocation under Ad Fatigue Constraints

#### What It Solves

Traditional attribution rewards the final touchpoint and ignores the hidden influence of preceding interactions. ROI Lens reconstructs the entire consumer journey, quantifies each channel's contribution, and identifies where marketing dollars truly create value.

#### Impact

Transform fragmented campaign data into actionable intelligence, optimize ₹100 Cr+ marketing investments, eliminate wasted spend, and engineer scalable growth strategies powered by data rather than assumptions.
